# This is for him, and for you maybe

**tmux** is a nice virtual thing for your terminal and helps you avoid madness of multiple windows. It bring you a bit of asciiart UI into your terminal and let you controll it via keyboard.

This will not be a full tutorial about tmux, becaus ethere are already many tutorials that will be better place to start with.

# Why tmux?

Because it's fully text UI.

Because I can do everything from keyboard.

Because I can cusomize it as much as I need whenever I need.

It holds my terminals mess in one place. Letting me display it as I need.


# Shortcuts

My standard activator i CTRL+B. For me this do two things. Works as an activator and if I double it - switch to previous pane.

What is an activator? This is a keyboard combination that tels tmux that you want to do something with it. Avoid here shortcuts from other apps to not get crazy when some one else will be reacting on your shot.

Than arrows! After activator I can use arrows to select different pane.

What pane is?

![Zrzut ekranu z 2026 11 15 18 04 46](/images/steam-powered-tools-for-modern-world-zrzut-ekranu-z-2026-11-15-18-04-46.png)

you can see here 3 panes that you can switch. I got binding set to **activator+H** for horizontal and **activator+V** for vertical split.

Here I also commonly use activator-C for create new window activator+N or P to select next/prev window.
